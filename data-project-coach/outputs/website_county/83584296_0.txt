Our Ethos
What we promise to families
Since IES was founded in 1993, the three pillars of our ethos have defined life in our schools:
Command of English: English is a global language and learning to command both the Swedish and English languages at an early age becomes hugely advantageous later in life. We believe fluency is best achieved through language immersion. Up to half of the education at Internationella Engelska Skolan is delivered through the English language, by native English-speaking teachers.
High academic expectations and aspirations: We are convinced that every child can achieve success irrespective of their background. This means we challenge every student to reach their full potential. We are dedicated to supporting students of every background to become responsible citizens and acquire the self-confidence and ambition to do the most with their talents.
A safe and calm school environment is a prerequisite for learning: We have clear structures and expectations as this helps students feel secure. This in turn is essential to a good learning environment and signals respect for the value of education. By showing respect for each other and ourselves we lay the foundations for safe and stimulating schooling.

Frequently Asked Questions and Contact
Find the answers to your questions
How do I apply to the school?
The application window is open all year round, and you can apply to multiple schools at the same time. We admit students on an ongoing basis throughout the year, subject to availability. To apply, please follow these steps:
Click on "Apply here".
Select the school you wish to apply to (each school has its own queue). You can place your child in the queue from February 1st the year after they were born.
Once you click "Apply here", you will be redirected to SchoolSoft’s queue system. Log in using BankID and fill in your personal details.
If you do not have a Swedish BankID, you can instead complete the application form available here.
I have applied to one school but want to join the queue for a different school instead – what should I do?
Each school has their own queue, and it is unfortunately not possible to transfer a queue position or accumulated queue time between schools. However, you are more than welcome to be in the queue for multiple schools simultaneously. You can easily add another school by logging into your SchoolSoft profile.
Does it cost anything to attend your school?
There are no fees to attend IES schools. Our schools are funded in the same way as those run by the municipality, through the Swedish tax system and the school voucher (skolpeng).
Are all your teachers qualified?
At IES schools, 85% of teachers are qualified (2024/2025). Roughly 6 out of 10 of these qualified in Sweden, while the remainder have teaching qualifications from abroad, mostly from English-speaking countries. We aim to recruit qualified teachers wherever possible. If we recruit an unqualified teacher, we put a development plan in place to set out how they will become qualified.
Swedish school law sets out that where teaching is conducted through the English language, those with a teaching degree from outside of Sweden are also qualified teachers. However, for teachers of English and modern foreign languages, additional requirements must be met to be considered qualified.
