INTERPRETATION AND DASHBOARD REQUIREMENTS
- Purposeful, non-representative sample; all three are independent schools in Stockholm municipality. No county-wide or causal conclusions.
- Website approaches are school self-descriptions, not verified practice. Retain excerpts, source URLs, retrieval dates and claim scopes from website_pilot_findings.md. No academic-versus-creative binary.
- Pupils per teacher FTE is NOT pupils per class. Class/group sizes were not found on the inspected website pages; no numeric class-size claim is supported. Approximate GR totals must not be divided by grades to estimate class sizes. Lilla Akademien's separate website total spans other educational stages.
- Display periods beside metrics. Year-6 2024/25 measures must not appear as 2025/26.
- Anne Frank register grades 1–6 versus website 4–6 remain unresolved. Empty year-9 arrays are retained as source status; current scope explains non-applicability, not historical performance.
- Qualification is in at least one teaching subject under the official definition, not every subject taught; check detailed exclusions before publication.
- Outcome differences may reflect cohort composition, admissions and other context. They do not demonstrate teaching effectiveness or superiority of an approach.
- Use side-by-side school cards with evidence links and explicit status badges, not an overall ranking. Rounded/flagged records must not enter exact-value calculations silently.
