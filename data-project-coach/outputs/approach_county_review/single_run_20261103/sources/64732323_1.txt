International Education and Dedicated Educators
Our goal is to provide a high-quality education to all our students. Our focus on a relational approach and our zero tolerance for bullying contribute to giving students the best possible start in their academic careers. We offer our students extended instruction in English from year 1 all the way up to year 8. Additionally, we provide teacher-led homework assistance.
Since August 2020, The Tanto international School has been part of Engelska Skolor och Förskolor i Sverige AB, which is owned by the Atvexa group whose vision is to create the best conditions for all children and students to grow.
