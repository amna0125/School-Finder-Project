Friends of BISS
Friends of BISS is a vibrant parent-led organisation that plays a key role in building community spirit across the school. Through events, initiatives, and volunteer support, they help create a warm, inclusive atmosphere for students, families, and staff alike.
Nurturing the active and caring BISS community
The Friends of BISS (FoB) is a vibrant volunteer organisation integral to the wider community at the British International School of Stockholm (BISS). All parents, legal guardians, and staff are automatically members of FoB, with an Executive Committee and a network of volunteers working to foster a strong sense of community and support at our school.
Executive Committee
The Executive Committee, which includes both staff and parent volunteers, oversees the activities and initiatives of FoB.
Class Parents
Each class has two designated Class Parents who assist in:
Cascading information to parents
Coordinating class events
Liaising between class teachers and parents on organisational matters
FoB Objectives
Promote Friendship and Communication:
Organise cultural and social events to foster positive relationships among parents, carers, and staff.
Facilitate Integration:
Provide a structured welcome system to help new families integrate into the school community.
Organize Student Events:
Plan age-appropriate social and special events for students.
Support School Needs:
Fundraise to obtain equipment and resources that benefit students and support FoB activities.
Support Charities:
Actively encourage and support the school’s chosen charities.
How You Can Help
Join the Executive Committee:
Contribute to planning and decision-making for FoB events.
Assist with Individual Events:
Help with tasks such as buying supplies, decorating, setting up, and managing event activities. Key events like Halloween and Christmas require assistance with party arrangements, competitions, and serving refreshments.
Contribute Financially:
Pay the subscription fee when requested. Funds are used to cover event costs, including decorations, treats, and other essentials.
Headline Events
Back to the BISS:
A welcome event at the start of the school year for all families.
Halloween:
Spooky competitions and costume celebrations.
Nobel Day:
A celebration of Alfred Nobel, the Swedish-born inventor and industrialist.
Christmas:
Festive activities and Santa’s workshops for our youngest students.
Teacher Appreciation Day:
A day to thank all BISS staff for their dedication.
International Day:
A celebration of our diverse international community.
Additional Events:
FoB also organises various cultural events and supports school events like welcome coffee mornings and sports day refreshments.
