About BISS
Discover BISS, Stockholm’s only COBIS-accredited British international school. Serving 380 students from 50+ nationalities since 1980.
When was the British International School of Stockholm founded?
The British International School of Stockholm was founded in 1980 as the British Primary School, beginning in Villa Borgen in Danderyd.
What started as a small school has grown into a thriving international community educating students for over 45 years.
Stockholm had limited options for British-style international education in 1980. Our founders created a school combining British educational traditions with an international outlook.
How has BISS grown since its founding?
Founded in 1980 as a small primary school, the British International School of Stockholm has expanded to serve approximately 380 students aged 3 to 18, representing more than 50 nationalities
. By the early 2000s, enrollment reached 200 students, prompting us to expand beyond primary education.
We added a Secondary section to serve growing families wanting their children to continue with us through university preparation. This expansion led us to adopt our current name, reflecting our evolution into a complete British International School.
Today we operate two campuses in Danderyd, just 15 minutes north of central Stockholm. Welcoming students from over 50 countries, we’ve built a genuinely global learning environment.
What major milestones has BISS achieved?
In 2019, the British International School of Stockholm became a full IB Diploma Programme school, celebrating our first IB graduates in 2021.
This authorization gave students access to one of the world’s most respected university preparation programmes.

What accreditations does BISS hold?
BISS is the only COBIS-accredited school in Sweden, and we’re an authorized IB World School and registered Cambridge International Education center.
These accreditations require independent quality verification from respected international organizations.
We’re one of only two Cambridge IGCSE schools in the Stockholm region, giving students access to internationally recognized qualifications not widely available in Sweden. Our Google Reference School status means all teachers and secondary students receive Chromebooks, supporting collaboration and digital fluency that universities and employers expect. Students compete annually in the COBIS Maths All Stars competition, testing their skills against peers at British international schools worldwide.
We are also proud members of the Nordic Network of International Schools, connecting us to leading schools across the Nordic and Baltic regions. This network enables collaboration, knowledge-sharing, and student participation in regional sports events and educational competitions.

Where have BISS graduates been accepted?
BISS graduates attend prestigious universities worldwide, including University of St. Andrews, University of Exeter, KTH Royal Institute of Technology and Lund University.
Our university outcomes demonstrate that students arrive prepared for academic success anywhere.
The diversity of destinations shows our students are prepared for whatever path they choose, wherever that takes them.
How has BISS’s educational philosophy evolved?
While rooted in the traditions of British education, The British International School of Stockholm has evolved to embrace global perspectives on learning.
Our journey from British Primary School to IB Diploma school represents growth, not abandonment of our foundations.
British educational tradition brings structure, strong academic foundations, and focus on well-rounded development. These values have remained constant since 1980. We’ve woven in the IB learner profile, emphasizing curiosity, critical thinking, and principled global citizenship.
What is BISS’s standing in 2025?
Today, BISS serves approximately 380 students aged 3-18 across two Danderyd campuses, entering an exciting chapter under School Director Corina Rader’s leadership since August 2025.
Our private international school model enables continuous investment in facilities, teachers, and programs. Since our first IB cohort graduated in 2021, we’ve built on results year after year. Beyond numbers, our community defines us – diverse, supportive, and focused on nurturing every child’s potential.
We’re building on 45 years of experience while focusing on what matters: preparing young people to thrive in an interconnected, rapidly changing world.
