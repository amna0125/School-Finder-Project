Our student care team works to promote good health and to help create the best conditions for students to thrive at school.  Lifestyle, health and learning are are seen as interconnected. The school health service continues the promotion of good health and preventative work that was begun at BVC. Our counselor contributes with the psychosocial perspective on the school through guidance on relationships, attitudes and conflict management and meets students in class and individually. We also have special educators, special teachers and social educators who work together with the counselor to bring together the academic and psychosocial perspectives in order to help the students in the best way.
Our safety and well-being group meets regularly and works towards a school without discrimination and for a good social and learning climate.
Our student care team consists of the principal, SEND coordinator, school nurse, counselor and special educator. A school psychologist and a school doctor are consulted regularly.
