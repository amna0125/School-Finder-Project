Our
E
ducation
Our goal is to offer a high-quality education to all our students. Our focus on a relational approach , small classes and our zero tolerance for bullying contribute to students getting the best possible start in their academic careers. Small classes provide the students with increased security and study focus.
Based on high teaching quality, the school attracts students who want to lay a good foundation for the future through stable knowledge of the English language ​​and a great understanding of a rapidly changing world. You also carry with you respect for differences and the values ​​of others without sacrificing democratic principles and humanity. All in all, the students are equipped for life with pride, self-confidence and a hopeful attitude. It is safe and fun to be a student at The Tanto International School.
N
ational Curriculum
The English National
curriculum provides students with the essential knowledge and skills to become well-educated, productive global citizens. Focused on core learning in English, Mathematics, Science, and Social Studies, we deliver stimulating lessons enhanced by the arts, digital world, and personal growth.
Education at Tanto goes beyond the classroom. Through museum and theatre visits, residential trips, and our Global Citizenship work, we create frequent opportunities for enrichment, helping students expand their experiences and foster a lifelong appreciation for creativity and achievement.
C
ore Learning
We are passionate about innovating education in core subjects, with English and Mathematics taught daily and Science playing a significant role in our learning opportunities. To deepen understanding, we integrate History and Geography into our English non-fiction units. This immersion allows students to engage with Social Studies concepts on a daily basis, helping them build strong, meaningful links between their literacy work and the world around them.
Digital World
While our students are "digital natives," we believe in using technology with purpose. We emphasize utilizing digital tools to enhance learning experiences without over-immersing our young children. By selecting the most appropriate moments for technology, we ensure it remains a powerful aid to our lessons rather than a distraction.
Our computing curriculum is built on the engaging
Purple Mash
platform, which focuses on progressive skill-building. Students master the basics before moving on to complex tasks through specially designed educational tools and games. To take learning further, our weekly
S.T.E.M. Lego lessons
bring robotics and engineering to life, offering some of the most creative and exciting opportunities for students to develop coding and control technology skills.
The Arts
The
Arts & Holistic Education
The Arts are an essential part of our weekly cu
rriculum, providing students with a wide range of opportunities to explore complex ideas and collaborate creatively. By integrating Art & Craft, Music, Dance, and Drama, we offer diverse ways for children to express themselves and build confidence.
We are dedicated to educating the "whole child." Regular engagement in these creative subjects, alongside Physical Education, fosters the expressive and cooperative skills that our students demonstrate throughout their school life. These weekly lessons are vital in ensuring that every child develops the self-assurance and creativity needed to thrive both in and out of the classroom.
Language & Culture
The Tanto International School is a vibrant, intercultural environment where we celebrate a community of over 20 different nationalities and languages. While many of our students speak multiple languages at home, they come together at our school to learn from each other's diverse perspectives and experiences every day.
What makes is unique among international schools in Stockholm is our immersive English environment. Unlike bilingual schools, our curriculum is taught entirely in English, providing a focused and consistent academic experience. However, we also value the language of our host country; Swedish is taught weekly through a wide range of activities and a dedicated reading scheme, ensuring that all children thrive and develop strong skills in the Swedish language.
Personal Growth
Personal Growth is at the heart of daily life at Tanto. We go beyond traditional subjects to engage students in their own thoughts, ideas, and understanding of the world. Through Class Council, P.S.H.E., and Global Perspectives, we support young people in building the confidence and resilience they need to take responsibility and demonstrate empathy for others.
Our approach is built on the
Tanto 3 C’s: Care, Collaborate, and Commit
. These values are woven into four key pillars of our school experience: Global Citizenship, P.S.H.E. Education, the Student Council, and our House Teams. Together, these elements ensure that our students grow into mindful individuals who understand their place and impact in a global society.
Enrichment
The Tanto International School has a long tradition of making the most of enriching educational activities for our students. Teachers facilitate regular opportunities to be inspired in a variety of fun and exciting ways inside and outside of the traditional classroom setting.
The school management believes that these opportunities form an essential part of a whole child's learning and can provide stimulus for other great teaching and learning activities.
School Library
For an equal school, all students should have access to literature and media as tools and through it be stimulated to use and develop their entire abilities.
Our school library also works to meet and promote the needs of multilingual students.
