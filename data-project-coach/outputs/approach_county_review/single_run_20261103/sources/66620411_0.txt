Welcome to the British International School of Stockholm
We cultivate an environment where mutual respect and understanding are paramount. Respect for others, their diverse backgrounds, and differing perspectives is integral to building positive relationships and a harmonious school community.
Learning & Thriving Together
Established in 1980, BISS is an all-through international school with students from over 50 countries aged 3-18 years. We are a learning-focused, student-centred school that places students at the centre of everything we do. Our holistic philosophy emphasises both the intellectual and personal development of our students. We provide a strong pastoral system, a broad curriculum and exciting enrichment opportunities to meet the needs of a diverse student population.
