Futuraskolan Hertig Karl in Sollentuna awarded the Grön Flagg prize for “Mest Hållbara Grundskola 2025” -Most Sustainable Primary School 2025! 🥳
The Swedish News for children and young learners, SVT’s “Lilla Aktuellt”, showcased the action and our students’ impressive work towards sustainability on Tuesday,  November 11th. Futuraskolan Hertig Karl’s students have been working hard this year to lessen food waste, recycle, reuse, and display the importance of taking care of our environment daily. They have come to understand that even small actions can and will make a big difference. We are so proud of all our students and staff at Futuraskolan Hertig Karl!
The CEO of Keep Sweden Clean, Johanna Ragnartz, writes: “By working systematically to strengthen students’ action skills, you are leading the way for quality sustainability education within Swedish schools. This creates the conditions for a future generation that wants, dares, and can participate in tackling the challenges of the future.”💚

About us
Futuraskolan International Hertig Karl is a small bilingual school with a global perspective and caters for Grade F-9. We work thematically interrelating the Swedish National Curriculum with the International Primary Curriculum (IPC) and the International Middle Years Curriculum (IMYC) creating a setting for you as a student to broaden your knowledge through a global perspective, gaining a deeper understanding of the world you live in. We view your academic and personal development as keys to our daily work.
Our staff has a vision:
We want to be the best stepping stone for future world-citizens.
We want to prepare you not only for Swedish society, but for a global start in life.
Our values ​​are characterized by
PER
.
Progressive
in that we, as a school, are at the forefront of research and development.
Energy
in that we take initiative, are creative, committed and see every problem as a challenge.
Respect
in that we are clear to each other about what is right and wrong behavior and making sure that our school is permeated with a democratic attitude and respect for our fellow human beings.
At our school there is one class per grade level from Förskoleklass (pre-school class) up to Grade 9. The school is located near Rotebro Centrum, Sollentuna, and is easy to get to by bus or commuter train.
We welcome you to Futuraskolan International Hertig Karl!

Idag har vi haft roligt besök av Busster och Mini Mobila som träffat alla våra elever som går i förskoleklass upp till årskurs 6.
Busster är en mobilfritidsverksamhet som drivs av Sollentuna kommun. De anordnar gratis aktiviteter för barn i åldrarna 7-12 år. Bussters husbil finns på Rotebro torg varje tisdag eftermiddag.
Mini mobila är en uppsökande verksamhet med fritidsledare som arbetar med olika aktiviteter på kommunens torg, parker och skolor och som också erbjuder kostnadsfria och trygga aktiviteter för barn i åldern 7-12 år.
English version
Today, we had a delightful visit from Busster and Mini Mobila, who met with all our students from preschool class up to grade 6.
Busster is a mobile after-school activity programme run by the Municipality of Sollentuna. They organize free activities for children aged 7–12. You can find the Busster motorhome at Rotebro Square every Tuesday afternoon.
Mini Mobila is also run by the Municipality of Sollentuna they also organize various activities in the municipality’s squares, parks, and school, offering free, fun and safe activities for children aged 7–12.
