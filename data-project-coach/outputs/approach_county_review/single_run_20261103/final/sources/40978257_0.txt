Excellence Starts Here – Futuraskolan International School of Stockholm.
Futuraskolan International School of Stockholm is a Grade 1-9 independent, non-sectarian, open admissions, co-educational day school located in central Stockholm. The school is designed to serve the children of the international community posted in Sweden and offers all classes in English, with the exception of modern language courses. The school year runs from mid-August to mid-June and has a minimum instructional period of 178 days divided into two semesters.
Futuraskolan International School of Stockholm, has a special permit from the Swedish authorities, as it is a school for temporary residents in Sweden and has a set of eligibility requirements. English is the language of instruction and it follows an international curriculum, not the Swedish National Curriculum. For more information on how to apply you are welcome to visit our
Admissions
page.
EAL –
We are deeply committed to supporting our EAL learners by providing tailored, differentiated instruction that empowers every student to thrive and succeed from day one. With small push-in and pull-out classes we focus on language development with links to curricular content
key words
and growing practical and academic vocabulary.
Swedish – With a focus on Swedish and connections to the host country, we lift up Swedish so that our learners, no matter where they are from or the length of the time that they are here, feel a connection to this country, the culture, and the Swedish language. Our learners gain proficiency and, in ninth grade if eligible, write the National Test in Swedish with our qualified Swedish teachers.
SYV –
Our in-house SYV team allows us to focus on high school and our learners, ensuring they receive a place in their English or Swedish school of Choice. We work actively with our learners and parents to help ensure the process is followed and understood. Our results speak for themselves June 2026 – 97% of learners were accepted to their first or second choice high school.

Our Grade 6 are starting the year off in a fun and meaningful way, helping to clean up the environment while on an overnight trip. Our learners helped volunteer scuba divers from our environmental partner, Hands2Ocean, clean up the marine environment around Waxholm by pulling objects, such as tires, metal and much more, out of the water.
