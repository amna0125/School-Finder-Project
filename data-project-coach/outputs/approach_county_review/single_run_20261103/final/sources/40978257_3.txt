Join our team
Thank you for considering Futuraskolan International School of Stockholm as a place to work. Working at Futuraskolan International School of Stockholm means you are joining a team of internationally minded, dynamic, progressive and enthusiastic staff members. Here you provide a valuable and innovative international education based on the International Primary Curriculum (IPC) and the International Middle Years Curriculum (IMYC) as well as the American Common Core Standards.
Aligned with the recommendations of the International Task Force on Child Protection, we hold ourselves to a high standard of effective recruiting practices with specific attention to child protection. Included in the recruitment process will be a local and national police check as well as a mandatory police background check from the last year of employment in every country worked.
Please visit the
Swedish Public Employment Office
and search “Futuraskolan” and you will find our available positions.
If you are interested in submitting your Letter of Interest and CV for our review and database, please email to Nicole MacDonald, Principal – nicole.macdonald@futuraskolan.se
