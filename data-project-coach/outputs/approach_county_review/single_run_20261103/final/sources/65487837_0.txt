About us
Welcome to Futuraskolan International Stockholm.
Futuraskolan International Stockholm is a bilingual school that offers education through Grades F – 9, we are for families who want their children to become internationally minded and experience a bilingual curriculum in English and Swedish.
Futuraskolan International Stockholm is a bilingual school, which means that our students’ lessons are conducted approximately 50% in English and 50% in Swedish. We also make sure that our teachers who work in English and Swedish have their working language as a native language. It is important that the students meet and use both languages ​​daily in different everyday situations, not just during class time.
We follow the Swedish National Curriculum and in addition to that, we also utilize the International Primary Curriculum (IPC) in Grades F – 5 and the International Middle Years Curriculum (IMYC) in Grades 6-9. With the IPC and IMYC, students are constantly developing and strengthening their skills as well as their knowledge and deeper understandings while broadening their world-wide perspective.
We welcome you to Futuraskolan International Stockholm!

What happens when curiosity meets microscopes, cell models, and a little genetic detective work? Our Grade 9 students found out! 🧪✨
Through our collaboration with Vetenskapens Hus, our young scientists stepped into a professional science environment to explore the fascinating world of cells and genetics — by getting hands-on!
🧫 Building life, one cell at a time! Students created their own plant and animal cell models and then zoomed in under the microscope to explore cellular structures.
🧬 Genes, traits & tiny changes! They investigated heredity, discovered how characteristics pass from one generation to the next, and explored genetic mutations and variation in species.
From building models to observing specimens and investigating scientific concepts firsthand, our students learned by doing, questioning, and discovering. Because science is so much more exciting when you can explore it for yourself! 🔍💡

🎨 What if the art begins before the painting?
What happens when you don’t just make art… but make the material that makes the art possible? 👀
As part of our “Scavengers and Settlers” unit, Grade 3 stepped back in time to explore the world of prehistoric cave art — with a twist!
🪨 No ready-made paint.
🥚 No skipping the science.
🎨 No limits to the experimenting!
Our young artists became artists + material scientists as they created their own egg tempera using egg yolk, yellow ochre pigment, water and a tiny splash of vinegar. They discovered that the pigment brings the colour, while the egg yolk acts as a binder, helping the pigment stick to the surface.
And then came the experiments… 🔬✨
By adding charcoal powder, students transformed the colour, creating beautiful muted, earthy tones inspired by the palette of prehistoric art.
The biggest discovery?
Art isn’t just about the picture at the end.
It’s about asking What if?
Trying. Changing. Observing. Problem-solving. Trying again. 💡
Because sometimes, before an artist can create a picture, they first have to create the material that makes the picture possible. 🖤🪨🎨
