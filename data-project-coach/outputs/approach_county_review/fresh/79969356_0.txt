Why Europaskolan?
Europaskolan was founded in 1998 by two teachers from Estonia. They believed that the education their children were receiving in Sweden could use an international approach.
After researching the various international educational bodies, it was decided the school would eventually be an IB world school. By 2007 the goal had been achieved. What began as a small school with 60 students and 6 employees grew into two schools with over 900 students and over 100 employees.
The passion and vision of the two founders has always been present in the school and their goal has always been to provide the best internationally inspired education possible to their students.
