A high-quality education is a gift for life
The Tanto International School offers students a high quality education in small classes. Our pedagogy is characterized by a sustainable approach where we strive to help each student realize their full potential both academically and as individuals. This is our gift to them. As they carry it forward into their future lives, it becomes their gift to us.
The Tanto International School is a part of Engelska Skolor och Förskolor i Sverige and Atvexa.
Professional Organisation
The Tanto International School requires that all teachers be able to speak high quality English. We take pride in the high educational level of our teachers.
We offer small classes, a safe environment, and personalised support where every child's success matters.
International Education
Our goal is to offer our children and students a high quality sustainable education. We know that a good and high-quality education increases the opportunities for a successful and fulfilling life.
We follow the Swedish curriculum in our preschool and the English curriculum with a global perspective in the primary school. Successfully guiding students through the Cambridge learning pathway to IGCSES.
Cultures Enrich Us
We are driven by the idea that different cultures enrich and strengthen us. Our intercultural approach permeates our daily work and contributes to a strong set of values ​​among both children, students and staff.
Good education is simply a good investment in the future.
