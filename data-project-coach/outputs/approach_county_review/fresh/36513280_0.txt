Futuraskolan AB is a private company that currently operates 13 pre-schools and schools in Stockholm and the Greater Stockholm region. 12 of these units follow the Swedish curriculum, seven of which are preschools and six of which are schools ranging from F – 9.
We pride ourselves on our International profile and supplement the Swedish National Curriculum Lpfö 18 and LGR22 with the international curriculum supplied by International Curriculum Association.
Our lessons are carried out in a bilingual manner, utilising both English and Swedish.
The 13th unit is our Grade 1-9 Futuraskolan International School of Stockholm. This school has a special permit from the Swedish authorities, as it is for temporary residents in Sweden. English is the language of instruction and it solely follows an International Curriculum. For more information about this school you can visit its separate website www.futuraskolaninternational.se

Day 4 - Learning Through Nature and Sustainability!
The day was filled with hands-on learning and meaningful experiences! a
Together with ELIX, our students travelled to the beautiful Imitos Mountains, where they worked in the botanical garden using different gardening tools. They got involved in removing weeds, trimming, cutting, carrying, and caring for the natural environment.
Through teamwork and practical tasks, they experienced how small actions can make a difference in protecting and maintaining our shared environment.
Afternoon Activity-The creativity took centre stage with a Tinkering Workshop with MIO!
Using waste collected the previous day, together with recyclable materials, the students worked in small groups to explore, experiment, build, and upcycle waste into something new.
Through the tinkering method, there was plenty of curiosity, problem-solving, collaboration, creativity, and -most importantly-learning by doing! O A wonderful day connecting nature, sustainability, teamwork, creativity and responsible citizenship.

Information from the Huvudman – Leadership Appointment - Futuraskolan International Preschool Brunbärsvägen – 2026-2027
The leadership team of Futuraskolan AB is thrilled to announce that Fareeha Rizwan has graciously agreed to assume the position of Principal of Futuraskolan International Preschool Brunbärsvägen. This decision is effective August 31, 2026
Fareeha came to Futuraskolan with a strong pedagogical education and immediately began making her mark. Initially assigned the coordinator role for our Global Citizenship Program at Futuraskolan International Preschool Brahegatan, Fareeha soon earned the appointment of Vice Principal at Futuraskolan International Preschool Gåshaga in 2018. While entrusted with leading the pedagogical development and systematic quality processes for the preschool for one year Fareeha earned the trust and respect of the entire parent community. Based on the outstanding academic and wellness results her and her team achieved, Fareeha was appointed Principal of Futuraskolan International Preschool Gåshaga in 2019. Upon her appointment she was placed in the states Rektorsprogrammet, which she completed in 2021.
While serving as Principal of Futuraskolan International Preschool Gåshaga, Fareeha has been the driving force behind the development of our Erasmus Global Exchange Program and our Global Citizenship Program`s Greece Project. Every year Futuraskolan facilitates a service learning trip for 140 grade 9 students in the organization. This program was created, developed and administered by Fareeha and the Global Citizenship Coordinators across the organization and is a shining example of the pedagogical aspirations that drive the development of Futuraskolan AB.
Fareeha’s leadership has resulted in student and teacher development across the organization. We are thrilled that Fareeha has agreed to lend Futuraskolan her immense talents in an expanded leadership role.
Please join us in welcoming Fareeha to her new role.
Tom Callahan
CEO Futuraskolan
