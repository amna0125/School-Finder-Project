IES Norrtälje – An International and Bilingual School in Norrtälje
Welcome to Internationella Engelska Skolan (IES) Norrtälje, a school that combines teaching in English and Swedish, with up to 50 % of lessons taught in English.
Here, students from grades F–7, and soon F–9, have the opportunity to develop their language skills and build a solid academic foundation that prepares them for the future in a globalized world.

600

Why Choose IES Norrtälje?
IES Norrtälje is located in the middle of Norrtälje in a safe environment, creating an inspiring place for learning and personal development. With dedicated teachers from around the world, we ensure that each student receives the attention and support they need to reach their full potential.
We take pride in our teaching methods and focus on discipline, respect, and academic ambition. Our students leave the school well-prepared for high school and further studies, equipped with the confidence to face future challenges.
